## Overview
The Injury Group is a New York–based personal injury law firm committed to protecting the rights of accident victims across all five boroughs and surrounding counties. 
We represent clients throughout Nassau County, Suffolk County, Queens, Kings County (Brooklyn), Manhattan, The Bronx, and Staten Island.

Our experienced attorneys specialize in car accidents, slip and fall injuries, construction site accidents, medical malpractice, and wrongful death cases. 
We focus on obtaining maximum compensation and providing compassionate, client-focused representation.
